SloppyYT Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

SloppyYT is a browser extension that labels videos on YouTube (for example "Slop", "Clickbait", "News") so you can decide what to watch. This policy explains exactly what data it handles.

What the extension sends

When you view a YouTube page, the extension reads the publicly visible text of the video tiles on that page: video ID, title, channel name and the metadata YouTube shows (view count, upload age, duration). It sends that text, plus a random install identifier generated when you install the extension, to the SloppyYT server to get a label for each video.

What it does not collect

  • No account, name, email, or YouTube login information.
  • No browsing history outside the video tiles shown on youtube.com.
  • No watch history, comments, cookies, or form data.
  • The extension does not run on any site other than youtube.com.

How data is used

Video text is used only to produce the labels. Labels are cached by video ID (not by user) for up to 14 days so the same public video is not classified twice. The install identifier and your IP address are used only to enforce a daily usage limit and are kept in daily counters that expire within about 25 hours.

Service providers

Classification runs on Vercel (hosting and AI Gateway) and the Jev model by TypeSafe AI. These providers process the video text only to return labels; per their published terms, the model is not trained on requests.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell or share data with anyone for advertising or any other purpose, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Local storage

Your settings and a local cache of labels are stored in your browser's extension storage. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the Support tab on the SloppyYT Chrome Web Store listing.
