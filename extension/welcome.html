SloppyYT is on.

Every video on YouTube now gets a colored box telling you what it really is, before you click.

What the colors mean

A dashed box means the model isn't sure. ⚡bait marks a clickbait-style title on an otherwise legit video. Hover a label to see the full breakdown.

Make it yours
  1. Pin SloppyYT: click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar, then the pin.
  2. Set any category to box, dim, hide or off. For example, hide slop and dim clickbait.
  3. Make your own categories, like "Drama", "True crime" or "Reaction videos". Describe them in plain English and SloppyYT sorts your feed by them.
